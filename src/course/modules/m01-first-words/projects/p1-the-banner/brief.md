## The situation

The door of the guildhall has no sign on it. Before anyone is let in to learn
anything, they are asked to make one — and to make it exactly right, because a
sign that is nearly right is a sign that sends people to the wrong door.

Four lines. No more, no less.

## What good looks like

```
+----------------------+
|  WELCOME, ADVENTURER |
+----------------------+
Your journey begins.
```

## Your objective

Print those four lines from the top level of your file. Not from inside a
function — just four statements, running one after another when the file runs.

Every character counts: the plus signs at the corners, the two spaces after the
opening bar, the full stop at the end.

## Watch out for

Each `print()` ends its line for you. You do not need `\n` at the end of any of
these — adding one gives you a blank line you did not ask for, and the check
will tell you so.
